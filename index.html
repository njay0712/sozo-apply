Before you apply

Make sure you meet the requirements and know what happens next.

Who can apply

  • You are between 16 and 35 years old
  • You have a South African ID, or a valid passport, asylum seeker permit or refugee ID. Foreign nationals are welcome.
  • You have a phone number where we can reach you
  • You can attend the full programme
  • You have not already applied for this intake. Only one application per person is accepted.
Registration fee: R100

What happens after you apply

  1. Complete the application and short assessmentAbout 15–20 minutes. The assessment helps us check that the school suits you. There are no trick questions.
  2. Save your reference numberYou will see it on screen straight away.
  3. We review applicationsOur team reviews every application after the closing date.
  4. Shortlisting and interviewsShortlisted applicants are contacted by phone and pay the R100 registration fee at the Sozo office.
  5. Final outcomeCheck your status on this website at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Applying is free. If you are shortlisted, a once-off R100 registration fee is payable in person at the Sozo office. Bring your reference number and your ID or document.

I am not South African. Can I apply?

Yes. You can apply with a valid passport, an asylum seeker permit (Section 22) or a refugee ID / refugee status document (Section 24). Please bring the same document to your interview.

Can I apply for more than one school?

No. Each person may submit one application for one school. This helps us contact every applicant fairly during callbacks. Read the school information carefully before you choose.

What is the assessment?

It is a short set of questions about the school you chose: what you enjoy, how you would handle real workplace situations, a few basic questions, and whether you can attend. You don't need experience. It helps us check that the training suits you.

I made a mistake on my application. What do I do?

You cannot apply a second time. Please contact the Sozo office with your reference number and we will correct it for you.

When will I hear back?

We review applications after the closing date on 28 January 2027. Shortlisted applicants are contacted by phone. You can check your status here at any time.

Why do you ask about income, grants and disability?

Our funders ask us to report on who our programmes reach, so we can keep the training free and accessible. Reports never include your name. These answers do not affect selection.
